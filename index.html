Who I am

About Me

I'm a Bachelor of Electrical Engineering Technology student at Higher Colleges of Technology in the UAE, on a full merit-based national scholarship — ranked among the top 273 students nationally in Ethiopia's Grade 12 leaving exams.

My work lives at the boundary of hardware intelligence and energy systems. I design embedded AI systems, model photovoltaic performance under real-world conditions, and probe the mathematical foundations of cryptographic randomness. All four of my IEEE papers were written completely independently — no supervisor, no lab, just obsession and late nights.

"The most interesting problems live where physics meets computation."

Long term, I'm building toward quantum computing hardware — the physical substrates that will define next-generation processing. Currently a Millennium Fellow through the UN Academic Impact programme, with 153+ logged volunteer hours.

Outside engineering: photography, basketball, drawing, and films that rewire how you see the world.
